I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at Harvard University, advised by Kosuke Imai. I work in causal inference, with a focus on policy evaluation and policy learning. A central part of my research is developing statistical decision theory based on counterfactual utility.

I have an ongoing research collaboration with Netflix, where I interned in the summer of 2025 in the Experimentation & Causal Inference group and in the summer of 2026 in the Machine Learning and Inference Research group.

Before joining Harvard, I earned degrees from the University of Oxford (MSc in Statistical Science), ETH Zurich (MSc in Applied Mathematics), and the University of Vienna (BSc in Mathematics). I also worked full-time as a Data Scientist at QuantCo, focusing on statistical machine learning and pricing. Prior to my academic journey, I served in the Austrian Armed Forces, where I trained to become an army officer.
